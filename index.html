      Keys

      • Drag a piece to move it. It snaps flush against walls and furniture closer than 4 cm.
      • R rotates 90°, Shift+R the other way
      • Arrow keys nudge 1 cm, with Shift 10 cm
      • Delete removes, Ctrl+D duplicates, Esc deselects
      • Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Shift+Z redo
      • Walk: WASD or arrows, drag to look

      Room sizes come from the floor plan. Where it gives no number, sizes are scaled from the drawing (about ±5 cm); the ceiling is assumed 2.60 m. Furniture is drawn as simplified shapes at real size. Built with three.js. Not affiliated with IKEA.
